
# Substrate Utilization (Fueling Insights)底物利用（供能洞察）

Estimated Fat and Carbohydrate Calories burned fields can be added to the workout Quick View for power-based cycling workouts. 基于功率的骑行训练中，估算的脂肪和碳水化合物卡路里消耗字段可以添加到训练快速查看中。

### What Are Fat and Carbohydrate Calories?什么是脂肪热量和碳水化合物热量？

Fats and Carbohydrates are two of the primary macronutrients the body uses as energy sources during exercise. 脂肪和碳水化合物是人体在运动过程中作为能量来源使用的两种主要常量营养素。
- Carbohydrates (Glycogen): Stored in muscles and the liver, glycogen is the body's quick energy source, which it increasingly draws upon as exercise intensity increases. Glycogen stores are limited and (for most people) can’t be fully replaced while exercising, which contributes to fatigue and a drop in sustainable exercise intensity.碳水化合物（糖原）：糖原储存在肌肉和肝脏中，是身体的快速能量来源，随着运动强度的增加，身体会越来越多地利用它。糖原储备是有限的，而且（对大多数人来说）在运动时无法完全补充，这会导致疲劳和可持续运动强度的下降。
- Fats: Fat is a more abundant energy source than carbohydrates in the body but takes longer to convert into energy than carbohydrates. The body burns more fat when exercise intensity is low. Training the body to burn fat efficiently saves glycogen stores for high-intensity efforts. 脂肪：脂肪是人体内比碳水化合物更丰富的能量来源，但转化为能量的时间比碳水化合物长。运动强度较低时，身体会燃烧更多脂肪。训练身体高效燃烧脂肪可以节省糖原储备，用于高强度运动。

By training the body to use both energy sources effectively, endurance and overall performance improve.通过训练身体有效利用这两种能量来源，耐力和整体表现都会得到提升。

### How Are Fat and Carbohydrate Calories Estimated?脂肪和碳水化合物热量是如何估算的？

TrainingPeaks estimates fat and carbohydrate calories based on:TrainingPeaks基于以下因素估算脂肪和碳水化合物的卡路里：
- Average power - As recorded by an athlete's device from their power meter.平均功率——由运动员设备通过其功率计记录。
- Weight - The athlete's last entered weight. 体重 - 运动员最近<bb1>输入</bb1>的体重。
- Gender - The gender chosen when the TrainingPeaks athlete account was created (Male/female) affects estimation.性别——创建TrainingPeaks运动员账户时选择的性别（男/女）会影响估算结果。
- Athlete Category (see below). 运动员类别（见下文）。

Using that information, TrainingPeaks estimates fat and carbohydrate calories burned on the ride using an algorithm created in collaboration with Dr. Iñigo San Millán.利用这些信息，TrainingPeaks通过与伊尼戈·圣米利安博士合作开发的算法，估算骑行过程中燃烧的脂肪和碳水化合物卡路里。

### How to Add Fat and Carb Fields 如何添加脂肪和碳水化合物字段

![image.png](attachments/431a39c4-c426-42ff-bf8b-70a33f42502f.png)

![https://help.trainingpeaks.com/hc/article_attachments/34580444339725](https://help.trainingpeaks.com/hc/article_attachments/34580444339725)

## Log in to your TrainingPeaks Coach (or athlete account).登录您的TrainingPeaks教练（或运动员账户）。

## Click your name. 点击您的姓名。

## Click settings. 点击设置。

## Click Layout. 点击布局。

## Scroll down to 'Workout' and select 'Bike.'向下滚动到“锻炼”并选择“自行车”。

## 'Available' and 'In Use' Fields will appear. “可用”和“使用中”字段将会显示。

## Drag Fat Calories and Carb Calories from 'Available' to 'In Use.'将“可用”中的脂肪卡路里和碳水化合物卡路里拖到“使用中”。

## Open a power-based cycling workout. 打开一个基于功率的骑行训练。

## In the quick view, you will see calculated Fat and Carb Calories. 在快速查看中，您将看到计算出的脂肪热量和碳水化合物热量。

Please note: 请注意：
- Fat and Carb fields do not appear in the Quick View card by default.默认情况下，“脂肪”和“碳水化合物”字段不会显示在快速查看卡片中。
- Adding the Fat and Carb fields into the Quick View changes what you (the coach) see, not what your athlete sees. If you want them to see those fields in their Quick View, you must enable them in their athlete settings.在快速查看中添加脂肪和碳水化合物字段会改变您（教练）所看到的内容，而不是您的运动员所看到的内容。如果您希望他们在自己的快速查看中看到这些字段，必须在他们的运动员设置中启用这些字段。
- If either of these fields (Fat or Carb) is enabled, the value in Calories (i.e., the total calories for the ride) will be the summation of fat kcal + carb kcal, as opposed to whatever the head unit reports back. If the fields are removed from available, the Calories field will revert back to what the device reports.如果这些字段（脂肪或碳水化合物）中的任何一个被启用，卡路里字段中的值（即骑行的总卡路里）将是脂肪千卡与碳水化合物千卡的总和，而非表头单元返回的任何数值。如果这些字段从可用状态中移除，卡路里字段将恢复为设备报告的数值。
- Also works for activity type 'Mountain Bike.'这也适用于“山地自行车”活动类型。

### Choosing a Metabolic Profile 选择代谢概况

![image.png](attachments/9ccaeaa3-ef77-4eea-b489-80ff5fca1ac5.png)

![36064595008653](attachments/c8e1eca1-8da3-4dd3-8d56-eb129b0e8016.png)

The Nutrition section within an athlete's setting houses the athlete's Metabolic Profile, which is a major variable in estimating Fat and Carb Calories. To access it:运动员环境中的营养部分包含运动员的代谢概况，这是估算脂肪和碳水化合物热量的一个主要变量。访问方法如下：

## Log in. 登录。

## Click your name. 点击你的名字。

## Click settings. 点击设置。

## On the left-hand side of the pop-up window, choose 'Nutrition.'在弹出窗口的左侧，选择“营养”。

## Choose the Metabolic Profile that most closely aligns with your athlete.选择与你的运动员最相符的代谢概况。
- World-Class- Professionals competing at the highest level (e.g. Olympics, Tour de France, etc).世界级——在最高水平赛事中竞技的专业人士（例如奥运会、环法自行车赛等）。
- Elite - National level elite and professional athletes competing at a very high competitive level.精英级——国家级精英和专业运动员，他们在极高的竞技水平上参赛。
- Competitive - Age group competitive athletes who participate in competitive events with a focus on performance.竞技类 - 参加竞技赛事并注重表现的年龄组竞技运动员。
- Enthusiast - Athletes who are relatively new to training or participate in events with the primary focus of completing events.爱好者 - 相对较新接触训练的运动员，或主要以完成赛事为目标参加赛事的运动员。
