
# 简易训练方案

### 简易训练方案

Lift x 2/week for 10-12 weeks. 3 sets, 4-10 reps/set of:  

## Squat  

## Leg press (one-sided)  

## Ankel plantar flexion  

## Standing hip flexion  

Then maintenance x1/week every 7-10 days
